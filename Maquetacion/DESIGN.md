## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-level martial arts mastery: disciplined, athletic, empowering, and undeniably premium. Built specifically for an elite female 2nd Degree Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Black Belt ('Professora'), the interface departs from aggressive combat sport tropes (blood splatters, distressed textures, neon sludge) in favor of precision, technical clarity, and sovereign authority.

The aesthetic fuses **Athletic Minimalism** with **Tactile Technical Depth**:
- **Discipline & Flow:** Clean geometric structure, razor-sharp alignment, generous structural breathing room, and controlled tension.
- **Empowerment without Cliches:** Steers clear of patronizing pastels. It commands respect through deep nautical oceanic foundations, electric cobalt kinetic highlights, and architectural charcoal contrasts.
- **Pedagogical Authority:** Martial arts credentialing, lineage verification, and belt progression systems are treated with the precision of high-end sports science and academic accreditation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive, nocturnal mat atmosphere with high-contrast tactical highlights.

### Functional Core
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0F19`):** Deep oceanic charcoal void that provides high contrast without the clinical glare of pure black.
- **Surface Elevation (`#111827`, `#1E293B`):** Slated layers representing physical floor mats, structural cards, and interactive planes.
- **Primary Energy (`#2563EB`):** High-voltage cobalt representing technical execution, focus, and dynamic kinetic movement.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#38BDF8`):** Glacial cyan used sparingly for focus rings, sub-indicators, micro-badges, and interactive hover states.
- **Action Highlight (`#22C55E`):** Distinct emerald reserved strictly for live WhatsApp conversion pathways and open schedule slots.

### Belt Progression Hierarchy
Subtle contextual accents honor traditional jiu-jitsu rank hierarchy within curriculum modules and student tracking components:
- **White (`#F8FAFC`):** Foundations, introductory sessions, trial cards.
- **Blue (`#3B82F6`):** Defense, survival mechanics, open mat booking.
- **Purple (`#A855F7`):** Combination chains, dynamic flow, intermediate seminars.
- **Brown (`#92400E`):** Pressure passing, submission refinement, advanced workshop tracks.
- **Black (`#18181B` with `#EF4444` tab):** Mastery masterclasses, instructor lineage, 2nd-degree certification badges.

## Typography

The typographical pairing pairs mechanical discipline with human warmth:
- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Conveys biomechanical precision, leverage, kinetic angles, and contemporary athleticism. Letter tracking is slightly tightened on larger display headers to enhance graphic density.
- **Body & Continuous Copy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers balanced geometry, clear legibility on dark canvases, and friendly, approachable counters that welcome beginners while preserving athletic rigor.
- **Labels, Badges, and Stats (Space Grotesk, Uppercase):** Evokes telemetry, round timers, and tournament scoring boards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm structured around a 12-column grid system.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width 1280px container, 12-column grid with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous 48px (`3rem`) screen margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins.

Vertical rhythm alternates between dense operational zones (curriculum schedules, pricing matrices) and broad breathing zones (hero transitions, philosophical quotes, lineage tree) using `space-2xl` increments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Tactile Tonal Layers** accented by **Electric Edge Luminance** rather than murky, unfocused drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0B0F19`, absorbing light and providing an expansive training floor feel.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces):** `#111827` overlaid with a hairline structural border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules / Menus):** `#1E293B` elevated with an ambient glow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.85), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.
- **Active State Glow:** Targeted primary action elements project a cobalt aura (`0 0 24px -2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.45)`) that conveys readiness and energy.
- **Glass Overlays:** Modals and sticky booking navigation employ background blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with a translucent midnight base (`rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern curvature:
- **Base Geometry (`0.5rem` / `rounded-md`):** Applied to inputs, micro-badges, timetable cells, and status indicators.
- **Large Cards & Content Panels (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`):** Delivers clean containment for coaching philosophies, trial packages, and technique breakdowns without looking juvenile.
- **Hero Framing & Modals (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`):** Gives primary imagery and hero promotional cards a tailored, contemporary feel.
- **Pill Geometry (Full Radius):** Reserved exclusively for dynamic status chips, belt milestone tags, and interactive conversion buttons.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Trial Class CTA:** Pill-shaped, electric cobalt background (`#2563EB`) with white typography (`Space Grotesk SemiBold`), elevated by a subtle forward arrow icon. Hover shifts to `#1D4ED8` with a diffused blue back-glow.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Dedicated pill button utilizing vivid emerald (`#22C55E`) paired with dark slate text (`#022C22`) or stark white, featuring the official WhatsApp icon. Emphasizes frictionless instant response for inquiries.
- **Secondary / Mat Outline:** Semi-transparent base (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), crisp 1px stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), transitioning on hover to solid white text with a luminous cyan outline (`#38BDF8`).

### Credential & Lineage Badges
- **Black Belt Degree Indicator:** Architectural rectangular tag with a deep onyx body, crimson rank bar (`#EF4444`), and twin silver stripes symbolizing 2nd Degree status.
- **Certification Tags:** Monospaced Space Grotesk tracking, enclosed in pill chips with 1px border tints matched to the specific achievement (IBJJF World Champion, SafeSport Certified, First Aid Certified).

### Cards & Curriculum Modules
- Deep slate surface (`#111827`) bounded by a subtle top-border highlight (`1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`).
- Visual division between program title, intensity gauge (1–5 mat icons), gi/no-gi indicator, and dedicated booking action.

### Interactive Class Timetable
- Tabbed interface segmented by Day and Experience Level (All Levels, Women Only, Advanced Competition).
- Cells display class status ("3 Spots Remaining", "Women Only", "Open Mat") using colored dot indicators.

### Form Inputs & Booking Flow
- Deep inset fields (`#0F172A`) with recessed inner shadows, explicit label positioning, and high-visibility cyan focus rings (`2px solid #38BDF8`) with zero layout shift.
